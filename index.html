Curbside privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Curbside is a single-player food truck game for Android and iOS, published by Insane Goblin Studios (Insane Goblin Studios Desenvolvimento de Software Ltda, São Paulo, Brazil).

Summary

Curbside does not collect, transmit or share personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress and settings are saved in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • The app keeps a short log of timings on the device, such as how long the game took to resume. It holds no personal information and is never sent anywhere.
  • If device backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android or iOS may include the game's saved progress in your own backup. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under your account; we have no access to it.

Notifications and vibration

The game can schedule reminders on your device, for example when a truck runs out of stock. They are created on the phone, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings. The app also uses vibration for touch feedback.

Purchases and ads

This version contains no advertising and no paid purchases. If a future version adds ads or paid purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Curbside does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
